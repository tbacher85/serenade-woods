Serenade Woods

Explore a lush pine forest solitude. Forage realistic 3D herbs & berries, craft wooden camp structures, light campfires that illuminate the dark night, toggle rain/fog weather, snap photographs, and gaze at brilliant star constellations.

Controls & Features

  • Move: WASD / Arrow Keys or Virtual Joystick
  • Look: Mouse Drag or Touch Swipe | Run: Hold Shift
  • Interact / Harvest: Press [E] near wood, realistic herbs, stream, tent, or fire
  • Crafting: Build Wooden Benches, Shelves, or Fences using collected wood
  • Camera: Press [C] to snap a photo into your in-game Album
