Serenade Woods

Cinematic 3D Forest & Camping Engine featuring Procedural PBR Normal/Roughness Maps, Rayleigh Atmospheric Sky, Vertex-Animated Wind Foliage, Dynamic Ripple Water, and UnrealBloom Post-Processing.

Controls & Features

  • Move: WASD / Arrow Keys or Virtual Joystick | Run: Hold Shift
  • Look: Mouse Drag or Touch Swipe
  • Interact / Harvest: Press [E] near wood, grounded herbs, stream, tent, or fire
  • Craft & Camp: Pitch tents, light blooming campfires, craft benches, brew herbal tea
  • Camera: Press [C] to capture cinematic photos into your in-game Album
