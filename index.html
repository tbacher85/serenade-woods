Serenade Woods

Step into a serene forest solitude. Wander through pine groves, forage wild medicinal herbs and berries, pitch your tent, build a campfire, boil tea, snap photographs, and gaze at starry constellations.

Controls & Gameplay

  • Move: WASD / Arrow Keys or Virtual Joystick
  • Look: Mouse Drag or Touch Swipe
  • Run: Hold Shift
  • Interact: Click on objects or press [E] near wood, herbs, stream, tent, or fire
  • Camera: Press [C] or click Photo to snap a picture
